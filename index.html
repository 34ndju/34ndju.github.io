I am a CS PhD student at UC Berkeley in the theory group, advised by Umesh Vazirani. My research interests are centered on quantum computation, with a particular focus on algorithms and complexity. Recently, I have been especially interested in algorithms for fermionic systems and complexity of many-body systems (e.g. area laws).

I gained my footing in research at UIUC, Los Alamos, and a wonderful summer at the IQC in Waterloo, with David Gosset.
